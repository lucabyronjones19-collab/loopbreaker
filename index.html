The Glitchy Alarm System

OCD is not a moral failing or a personality trait. It is a biological glitch where the brain's amygdala triggers a false "Code Red" survival response to random data. The threat feels 100% real, but the alarm itself is completely empty.

The Reassurance Trap

When an intrusive thought strikes, your brain demands 100% certainty ("Am I safe?", "Am I a good person?"). Trying to answer this or using mental tricks to fix it acts as a reward for the glitch, causing it to return stronger every 3 minutes.

The Core Cure: ERP

Exposure and Response Prevention means letting the scary thought sit in your mind completely unmanaged. By delaying your reaction and refusing to perform a mental ritual, you train your brain to realize that the danger is artificial.
